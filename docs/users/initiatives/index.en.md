# Initiative overview

<!-- md:version 3.0.0 -->
<!-- md:feature -->

Initiatives and objectives are available from **OSIRIS 3.0.0**, initially in beta. Your institute may call them **missions**, **working groups** or something else. This guide uses the default names. The [comparison of the three organisational structures](../../topics/organizational-structure.md) explains how they differ from research areas and organisational units.

## Open the section

Open **Initiatives** in the sidebar. The compass icon helps identify it if your institute uses another name. Initiatives and objectives are also accessible through the command palette. If the section is missing, ask your administrator: the feature must be enabled and your role must have access.

The overview offers **Current**, **Past and archived**, and **All**. **Objectives** opens the combined objective overview; it appears once objectives exist.

## From an initiative to its contents

Click an initiative's name. Its detail page shows the configured information, period and status. Below are its objectives and linked contents, such as publications, other activities, projects and proposals. Only contents you are allowed to access are shown.

Click an objective to open its own detail page. It shows its information and only contents linked to that objective. The parent initiative link takes you back to the broader context.

Initiatives can also be used without objectives. If your personal membership is explicitly maintained on an initiative, it can also appear in your profile's **Affiliations**. Contributing to a linked activity is separate from membership.
