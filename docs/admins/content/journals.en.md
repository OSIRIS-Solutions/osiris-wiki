# Journals

<!-- md:version 2.1.0 -->

On this page, you can customize the template for the information pages of your journals. You can change the name of this page in the navigation bar and choose whether **Journal Metrics** should be downloaded automatically.

Please note that the metrics are provided by Scimago and are based on Scopus data. If you prefer to use different impact factors or quartiles, you can disable the automatic import. In that case, you will need to maintain the data manually.

You can also customize the name of the **main metrics field**.

In addition, you can add **custom fields** to your journal pages, for example to store APC costs, currencies, transformative agreements, or internal classifications. These fields can be managed in the Journal Editor and are displayed in the journal's detail view.
